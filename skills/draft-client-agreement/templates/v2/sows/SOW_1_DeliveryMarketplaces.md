## STATEMENT OF WORK: DELIVERY MARKETPLACES (RECURRING SERVICE)

Header, Term, Payment, and Signature per `00_SHARED-BOILERPLATE.md`.

### 2.1 Scope of services
Spice Digital continuously manages the Client's third-party delivery marketplace
presence on Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Grubhub. Service extends solely to the
locations listed in the Monthly Rate Summary. A one-week onboarding period applies
at the start of the engagement and whenever a service is added to the account.

### 2.2 Deliverables
- Delivery platform diagnostic & action plan
- Menu management: category structure, item descriptions, photography review, seasonal updates, 86'd-item management
- Storefront optimization: hero images, store info, hours, delivery radius, ratings management
- Promotional strategy and campaign management (platform offers, seasonal promotions)
- Ad campaign management on DoorDash and Uber Eats marketplace ads
- Support-ticket and escalation management across all three platforms
- Account-manager relationship management across all platforms
- Weekly performance reporting (organic vs. marketing-driven sales) via Spice's reporting stack
- Weekly or bi-weekly client sync

### 2.3 Pricing matrix — 5-location minimum; base covers the first 5 locations
Client selects **one track** at signing. Track choice is revisited only at renewal.

**Track A — Flat**

| Package | Locations | Monthly fee |
|---|---|---|
| Base service fee | Covers first 5 | $2,750 |
| Additional locations | 6+ | $175 per location |
| Menu updates | All | $50/hour (not billed monthly) |
| Design work | Project-based | $75/hour (not billed monthly) |

**Track B — Performance** *(offered only if the client clears the discovery gate:
≥ $125K/mo top-line delivery, ~$1.5M/yr, or ≥ $75K/mo net payout if shared)*

| Package | Locations | Monthly fee |
|---|---|---|
| Base service fee | Covers first 5 | $1,750 |
| Additional locations | 6+ | $100 per location |
| Performance incentive | — | 10% of net-payout dollars above (baseline + 5%), summed across UE/DD/GH |

**Track B mechanics.** Metric = net-payout dollars (after platform fees and
discounts). Baseline = same-month-prior-year payout, locked at signing; segments
without 12 months of history start on a trailing-90-day launch baseline and
graduate to the prior-year basis at month 12. First 5% of growth over baseline is
free; 10% of payout dollars above that threshold, trued up monthly, billed the
following month, re-based at renewal. **Same-store basis** — only stores in the
baseline set at signing count; new stores join the baseline set at their 12-month
mark.
