## STATEMENT OF WORK: ezCATER CATERING MANAGEMENT (RECURRING SERVICE)

Header, Term, Payment, and Signature per `00_SHARED-BOILERPLATE.md`.

### 2.1 Scope of services (v1 — marketing / visibility layer)
Spice Digital manages the Client's ezCater catering marketplace presence across
both ezCater surfaces (Partner Portal and ezManage). Service extends to the
locations listed in the Monthly Rate Summary. Monthly cadence. A one-week
onboarding period applies.

### 2.2 Deliverables
- Kickoff diagnostic: every store scored on Ops · Visibility · Packaging; badge funnel; tiered action plan
- Reliability & pause management (cancellations ≤3%, rejections ≤5%, on-time ≥95%, ready-for-dispatch ≥95%); remediation to reinstate paused stores
- Reliability Rockstar badge pursuit against the six goals (including delivery-tracking ≥75%)
- Visibility / paid levers via ezManage: Preferred Partner bidding, ezRewards rate, sponsored listings, promotions
- Menu & conversion: photos, packaging (per-person pricing, headcount tiers, Feeds-10/25/50 bundles), pricing to lift menu-view→order conversion
- Monthly management report: sales funnel, customer mix, badge/reliability status, lever activity
- Quarterly review: re-diagnose + lever roadmap

**Out of scope (v1):** corporate-account sales outreach, lead-time/capacity
consulting, ezDispatch logistics operations.

### 2.3 Pricing matrix — 5-location minimum; base covers the first 5 locations
Client selects **one track** at signing.

**Track A — Flat**

| Package | Locations | Monthly fee |
|---|---|---|
| Base fee | Covers first 5 | $1,250 |
| Additional locations | 6–10 | $150 per location |
| Additional locations | 11+ | $75 per location |
| 50+ locations | — | Custom enterprise quote |

**Track B — Performance** *(offered only if the client clears the discovery gate:
≥ $65K/mo ezCater sales, ~$780K/yr, or ≥ $50K/mo payout if shared)*

| Package | Locations | Monthly fee |
|---|---|---|
| Base fee | Covers first 5 | $750 |
| Additional locations | 6–10 | $100 per location |
| Additional locations | 11+ | $50 per location |
| Performance incentive | — | 10% of net-payout dollars above (baseline + 5%) |

**Track B mechanics.** Metric = ezCater net payout (after fees and discounts).
Baseline = same-month-prior-year payout locked at signing; trailing-90-day launch
baseline where no prior-year history exists, graduating at month 12. First 5% of
growth free; 10% above threshold, trued up monthly, re-based at renewal.
