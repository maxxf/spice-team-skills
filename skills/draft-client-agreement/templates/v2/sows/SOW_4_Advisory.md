### 2.1 Scope of services
Spice Digital provides strategic advisory as a layer on top of the Client's active
operational engagement(s). Advisory is provided by Maxx Freedman directly. This SOW
is valid only alongside at least one active operational SOW; it does not stand
alone.

### 2.2 Deliverables (by tier)

**Advisory-Lite — $2,500/mo**
- 1-hour strategy call every 2 weeks with Maxx
- Cross-service coordination across the Client's active Spice engagements
- Quarterly strategic review and roadmap

- Platform relationship introductions; competitive intelligence briefings

*Not included:* execution of recommendations (delivered by the operational
services), weekly cadence, direct management of the Client's internal team.

**Fractional Head of Growth — $5,000/mo**

- Cross-channel strategy (delivery, retention, catering, paid, content, partnerships)
- Direct coordination with the Spice ops team on execution priorities
- Senior-level platform relationship management
- P&L review and margin-optimization strategy; new-market launch planning
- Vendor evaluation and recommendations
- Email and Slack access

*Not included:* in-person presence, direct management of the Client's internal
team (advisory, not management), execution beyond what the operational services
provide.
