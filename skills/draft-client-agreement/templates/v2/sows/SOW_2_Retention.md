## STATEMENT OF WORK: RETENTION MARKETING (RECURRING SERVICE)

Header, Term, Payment, and Signature per `00_SHARED-BOILERPLATE.md`.

### 2.1 Scope of services
Spice Digital designs, builds, and executes the Client's first-party retention
program — automated flows and recurring campaigns — on the Client's existing
retention platform (Toast, Thanx, or Klaviyo). Service extends to the locations
listed in the Monthly Rate Summary. A one-week onboarding period applies.

### 2.2 Deliverables (by tier)
| | Basic — $1,000/mo | Standard — $1,750/mo | Pro — $2,500/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | 1–5 locations | 6–15 locations | 15+ locations |
| Campaigns/mo | 2 | 4 | 4 |
| Planning | Client-led; Client submits approved campaigns 30 days before send | Collaborative; 1 monthly planning session, Spice recommends, Client approves | Strategic partnership; 2 monthly sessions, Spice leads + quarterly roadmap |
| Testing | — | — | A/B, advanced segmentation, multivariate |
| Deliverables | Copy, design, scheduling, monthly KPI snapshot | + monthly planning session, campaign reporting, optimization plan | + continuous optimization |
| Revisions | 1× per campaign | 2× per campaign | 2× per campaign |

### 2.3 One-time setup — $3,500 (waived on 6-month commitment)
Four automated flows (welcome, winback, first-to-second order, loyalty), brand
library design, and five campaign templates.

### 2.4 SMS add-on (optional) — $500/mo
Attaches only to an active retention tier on the same platform as the email
program.
- Up to 4 SMS automations (cloned from the live email flows) + 2 SMS campaigns/mo, synced to the email calendar.
- 10DLC carrier registration, opt-in language, and consent flow — owned by Spice, non-negotiable.
- **SMS activation $500 one-time** (waived on a 6-month add-on commitment).
- Additional SMS campaign $200 each beyond 2/mo. Per-message carrier fees are pass-through, billed to the Client by the platform.
- Co-terms with the base retention engagement.

### 2.5 Add-ons & overages
- Additional email campaign: $375 each.
- Loyalty program build/management: separate scope (not included).
