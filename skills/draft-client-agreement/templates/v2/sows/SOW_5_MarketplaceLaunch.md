## STATEMENT OF WORK: MARKETPLACE LAUNCH PACKAGE

Header, Payment method, and Signature per `00_SHARED-BOILERPLATE.md`.

### 2.1 When this SOW applies
For brands not live (or effectively dark) on the managed marketplaces:
new-to-delivery brands, brands returning after delisting, first-time 3P launches.
An existing managed client adding a single platform stays in ongoing Delivery
Marketplaces scope and does not use this package. 5-location minimum.

### 2.2 Scope of services
A fixed-scope launch build delivered in three phases, followed by ongoing Delivery
Marketplaces management beginning at go-live.

**Phase 1 — Strategy + Leverage (weeks 1–4)**
- Platform sequencing strategy (dominant platform first)
- Commission rate negotiation while leverage is highest, before the brand signs
- Account creation on all launch platforms, rep introductions
- Curated launch menu strategy built for conversion and ratings
- Launch announcement plan for the brand's owned channels

**Phase 2 — Build + Test (weeks 5–8)**
- Menu builds on every platform (structure, descriptions, photography review, platform-specific pricing)
- POS integration setup (Toast and equivalents)
- Test orders at every location
- Storefront setup (hero images, store info, hours, delivery radius)

**Phase 3 — Go-live + Launch Window (weeks 9–12+)**
- Go-live management on the first platform
- 30-day algorithm-window management: ratings defense, conversion optimization, driving owned traffic
- Launch announcements executed
- Subsequent platform go-lives handled inside ongoing management per the sequencing plan

### 2.3 Pricing — one-time launch fee (milestone billed)
| Component | Price |
|---|---|
| Launch package (covers first 5 locations) | $12,000 one-time |
| Additional locations | $500 per location beyond 5 |
| Ongoing management | Starts at go-live, Delivery Marketplaces Track A ($2,750/mo base) |

**Milestone billing (deliverables, not retainer):**
1. **40% ($4,800) at signing** — launch plan locked, accounts created, rep intros made, commission negotiation opened
2. **30% ($3,600) at build-complete** — menus built on all launch platforms, POS integration connected, test orders passed at every location
3. **30% ($3,600) at go-live** — first platform live plus the 30-day launch window managed

The launch package is **not waivable** — it is the product, not a fee.

### 2.4 Term (service-specific)
The 6-month ongoing-management minimum begins **at go-live**, not at the Effective
Date. Typical total engagement is ~9 months (build + 6-month ongoing). Optional
lever: if the Client commits to a **12-month ongoing minimum at signing**, Spice
credits **$2,000** against the launch package.

### 2.5 Track note
Launch clients have no delivery baseline, so ongoing management is **Track A only**
at signing. Track B may be offered at first renewal once history exists and the
discovery gate clears.
