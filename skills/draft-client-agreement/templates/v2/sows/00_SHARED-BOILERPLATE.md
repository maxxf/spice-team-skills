# Shared Boilerplate — applies to every SOW

**Draft for legal review.** This is the common language each service SOW inherits.
In the assembled agreement, the Term / Termination / Payment provisions live once
(in the MSA and this shared block) rather than being repeated in every SOW.

---

## Term & Termination (centralized — governs all attached SOWs)

**Initial Term.** Each engagement begins on the Effective Date and continues for a
fixed **six (6) months** (the "Initial Term"). The Initial Term is fixed upon
execution; Client is responsible for Fees for the entire Initial Term. (For the
Marketplace Launch SOW, the six-month ongoing-management clock begins at go-live,
not at the Effective Date — see that SOW.)

**Continuation.** No automatic renewal. After the Initial Term, services continue
month-to-month.

**Termination for convenience (continuation only).** During any month-to-month
continuation after the Initial Term, either Party may terminate for convenience on
**sixty (60) days'** written notice.

**Termination for cause.** Spice Digital may suspend or terminate on ten (10)
days' written notice if any undisputed Fees remain unpaid after the notice period.

| Contract period | Notice of termination | Fees payable |
|---|---|---|
| Initial Term — 6 months | Fixed upon execution. Cannot be terminated for convenience. | All costs and Monthly Fees for the Initial Term. |
| Continuation (month-to-month) | Anytime, on 60 days' written notice. | Costs and Monthly Fees up to the Termination Date. |

**Adding / removing services.** A service module may be added mid-term via a new
SOW; adding a module does not reset the Initial Term clock, and the new module's
first month is prorated. Removing a service requires 30 days' written notice and
may not reduce the engagement below the minimum viable package during the Initial
Term. Adding, replacing, or removing locations requires a new or amended SOW with
adjusted Fees, confirmed in writing by email (Slack or verbal is insufficient) and
naming the specific locations.

## Fees & Payment (centralized)

**Monthly Fees.** Fees are due at the start of each term and auto-charged each
billing cycle to the payment method on file, on or about the first (1st) of the
month. Fees may be prorated for partial months.

**One-time Fees.** Any one-time Fees (e.g., Retention setup, SMS activation,
Launch milestones) are billed per the schedule in the applicable SOW.

**Payment method.** Auto-pay via ACH or credit card is required. Late balances
accrue 1.5% monthly interest; services may be suspended after 30 days past due.

**Standard exclusions (all services).** Platform commission fees; ad spend
(pass-through, billed separately); third-party software fees; photography /
videography production; menu printing or physical collateral.
