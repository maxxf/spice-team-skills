## Voice in one line
Bold, cheeky, unapologetically big — leans hard into the "Big Fat" name with macro-flexed swagger.

**Tagline-style copy:**

**CTA verbs:** Build Your High-Protein Bowl · Get Your Free Rewards! · Order Now

## Vocabulary (use these terms verbatim when relevant)
- **Big Fat [thing]** — leans into the name. Big Fat Rewards, Big Fat Way, Big Fat Protein Party.
- **Spicy Fat Sauce** — signature sauce, brand asset
- **Saffron Yellow Rice** — signature base
- **Chicken Shawarma**, **Beef Filet**, **Crispy Chicken**, **Falafel** — protein options (always proper case)
- **KNOW YOUR BOWL** — recurring header for build/education emails
- **BASE / PROTEIN / TOPPINGS / SAUCE / EXTRAS** — the build framework, all caps when listed

## Tone rules
- **Lean into the name.** "Big Fat" is owned. Do not soften it. Do not avoid it.
- **Macros + flavor are not in conflict.** "high-protein" + "actually taste like something to feel guilty about." Both at once.
- **All-caps for emphasis** in headers and section labels (KNOW YOUR BOWL, BASE, etc.). Never in body copy.
- **Emoji ration:** 🎉 🏋️ 🥗 🔥 are all on-brand. 2-3 per email is fine here (more than HealthNut/Ahipoki).
- **Customization = power.** Same as Ahipoki, but framed around macros/goals: "tracking macros, eating cleaner, or just trying to feel better."
- **First-order hook:** "free side when you download the app" is a recurring offer.

## Recurring campaign types
- **Macro / fitness positioning** — high-protein bowls, custom macro builds, fuel your goals
- **Bold / flavor-forward** — "Big Fat" energy, spicy callouts, indulgence-meets-clean-eating
- **Seasonal / event** — Superbowl Campaign, holiday hooks, sports-tied promos
- **Automation flows** — welcome, win-back, review request (already set up, brief these only when refresh is needed)

## SMS pattern (MBFS DOES use SMS — 2/month)
- Punchier than HealthNut SMS — leans into the name
- 1-2 sentences max, ends with `→ \[link\]`
- Example: `New high-protein bowls just hit. Built your way, the Big Fat Way → \[link\]`

## What to avoid
- **Don't shy from "fat."** It's the brand. Owning it is the entire voice.
- Wellness/clean-eating language without the wink. MBFS is health-adjacent but cheeky, not earnest.
- Generic shawarma descriptions ("authentic Middle Eastern flavors"). The voice is Big Fat-coded, not heritage-coded.
- Long subject lines. Punchier > descriptive.
