# Ahipoki — Voice & Style Guide

## Account snapshot
- **Locations:** 26, across CA + AZ
- **Service tier:** Retention Lite x4 campaigns/month (Email only — NO SMS in scope)
- **Loyalty:** Thanx-powered
- **Order link:** https://order.thanx.com/ahipoki
- **Bowl format:** 2 or 3 scoops, build-your-own (base → protein → sauce → toppings)

## Voice in one line
Helpful, warm, a little surf-shack — the friend who walks you through your first poke order.

## Voice signals (extracted from production briefs)

**Subject lines that have run:**
- `Your First Poke Bowl: A Guide 🥢`
- `Thanksgiving, The Ahipoki Way`

**Opening hooks:**
- "Never had poke before? Start here"
- "New here? Try these first-timer favorites"

**CTA verbs:** Start Building · Order Your First Bowl · Build Your Bowl

**Reassurance-style copy:**
- "Can't decide? Our team is always happy to make recommendations."
- "You really can't go wrong."
- "We got you 🥢"

## Vocabulary (use these terms verbatim when relevant)
- **Bowl** — the unit of order (NOT meal, NOT plate)
- **Scoops** — protein quantity (2 or 3)
- **Base** — rice, greens, the foundation
- **The Classic** — Ahi tuna + white rice + house sauce + cucumber & avocado (signature combo)
- **Hawaiian BBQ chicken**, **Spicy creamy** — distinctive sauce/protein names
- **Build Your Poke Bowl** — the 1-2-3-4 menu infographic, brand asset

## Signature builds (call out by name when relevant)
- **The Classic** — Ahi tuna + white rice + house sauce + cucumber & avocado
- **Fresh & Mild** — Salmon + organic greens + sweet citrus + edamame & seaweed salad
- **Grilled & Safe** — Hawaiian BBQ chicken + brown rice + spicy creamy + green onions & sesame seeds

## Tone rules
- **Educate without condescending.** First-timers are common — lean into it.
- **Specifics over vagueness.** Don't say "fresh ingredients", say "Ahi tuna + house sauce + cucumber & avocado". Names sell.
- **Emoji ration:** the chopsticks 🥢, fish 🐟, and pineapple 🍍 are on-brand. 1 per email is plenty.
- **Customization = empowerment.** Always reinforce that the customer is in charge.
- **Holiday angles play well.** "The Ahipoki Way" framing for any seasonal moment (Thanksgiving, Cinco de Mayo, Mother's Day).

## Recurring campaign types
- **Education / first-timer** — how to order, build-a-bowl walkthrough, signature builds
- **Holiday / seasonal** — "[Holiday], The Ahipoki Way"
- **Anniversary / milestone** — store openings, brand birthdays
- **Menu / LTO** — new sauces, new proteins, limited-time bowls

## PUSH notifications (Ahipoki uses Thanx push)
- Tighter than SMS — single sentence + emoji
- Often a teaser that funnels to the email
- Example: `Never had poke? We got you 🥢 Your first bowl is easier than you think. Pick your base, pick your protein, pick your toppings — done.`
- Render in brief as `**PUSH:** [copy]` with gray_bg callout

## What to avoid
- **NO SMS.** Ahipoki is on the lite plan and doesn't have SMS in scope. Don't propose it.
- Cliché tropical language ("paradise in a bowl", "island vibes"). Too on-the-nose.
- Over-fishing the chopstick emoji. Once per email max.
- Lecturing on health benefits. Ahipoki is poke, not a wellness brand.
