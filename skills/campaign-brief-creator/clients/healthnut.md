# Health Nut — Voice & Style Guide

## Account snapshot
- **Locations:** 5, all in LA
- **Service tier:** Retention Lite (2 campaigns/month + SMS via Thanx)
- **Loyalty:** Thanx-powered, points called **Leaves**, items called rewards
- **Order link:** https://order.thanx.com/healthnut
- **Loyalty link:** https://healthnutla.com/loyalty-rewards/

## Voice in one line
Fresh, friendly, slightly playful — like the friend who always knows the lunch spot.

## Voice signals (extracted from production briefs)

**Subject lines that have run:**
- `Double Leaves is back 🌿🌿`
- `NEW Rewards just dropped in the marketplace! 🎉`

**Opening hooks:**
- "Your favorite week is back."
- "We just added [reward name] to the Health Nut Rewards marketplace!"

**CTA verbs:** Order & Earn Double · See My Rewards · Join & Get My Free Drink · Redeem Now · View All Rewards

**Sign-off-style closers:**
- "Don't let it pass."
- "this is the week to stack up"

## Vocabulary (use these terms verbatim when relevant)
- **Leaves** — the loyalty currency (NOT points, NOT credits)
- **Marketplace** — the rewards section in the app
- **Noodlerama** — popular reward item, recognizable to members
- **Mango Greentini** — signature drink, often used in sign-up offers
- **Free Scoop of Tuna**, **Free Small Drink** — common low-tier rewards

## Tone rules
- **Casual, not corporate.** Contractions everywhere. "you're", "don't", "it's".
- **Math-as-payoff.** "Earn 2x" → "you just got there twice as fast." Show the ROI of the action.
- **Emoji ration:** 1-2 per email max. The leaf 🌿 is the brand mark, use sparingly.
- **No exclamation point overuse.** Energy comes from sentence structure, not punctuation.
- **Reward stacking framing.** Members earn toward something specific (free drink, free scoop). Always name the next unlock when possible.

## Recurring campaign types
- **Loyalty engagement** — Double Leaves Week, points multipliers, milestone callouts
- **Marketplace updates** — new rewards, expiring rewards, FOMO-driven launches
- **First-time / acquisition** — sign-up + free Mango Greentini hook

## SMS pattern (HealthNut DOES use SMS via Thanx)
- 1-2 sentences max
- Lead with offer or hook, no preamble
- End with `→ \[link\]`
- Example: `Double Leaves is BACK 🌿🌿 Earn 2x on every order 4/20–4/25. Those new rewards aren't gonna unlock themselves → \[link\]`

## What to avoid
- Generic "wellness" language ("nourish your body", "fuel your day"). Health Nut is health-adjacent but doesn't lecture.
- Over-explaining the loyalty program — members already know how Leaves work.
- Long block paragraphs. Keep copy scan-able.

## Quick subject line pattern bank
- "[Thing] is back 🌿🌿" (return-of-favorite frame)
- "NEW [thing] just dropped" (announcement frame)
- "[Number] new rewards, just for you" (ownership frame)
- "Your favorite [day/week/perk] is here" (anticipation frame)
